# TRYIT

Use the TRYIT tools when the user asks about short-form video trends, viral content, rising creators or keywords, a YouTube channel's performance, where a clip first appeared, or which ads ran the longest.

- `account_status` shows the credit balance; `estimate_cost` lists prices.
- `search_content` takes a topic plus optional `country` (ISO code) and `platform`.
- `similar_content` and `find_original` accept `source` + `id` from a `search_content` result.
- `analyze_channel` needs a YouTube channel id (UC…).
- Views, subscribers and keyword counts are observed public values; scores, grades and viral multiples are TRYIT estimates.
- `find_original` returns candidates, not a rights or ownership decision.
